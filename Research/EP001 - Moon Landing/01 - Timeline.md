# EP001 - Moon Landing - Timeline

## Mission

**Apollo 11**

**Crew:**
- Neil Armstrong - Commander
- Edwin "Buzz" Aldrin - Lunar Module Pilot
- Michael Collins - Command Module Pilot

**Launch:** July 16, 1969  
**Lunar Landing:** July 20, 1969  
**Splashdown:** July 24, 1969

**Mission Duration:** 195 hours, 18 minutes, 35 seconds

---

# Pre-Mission Context

## May 25, 1961

President John F. Kennedy announces the national goal of landing a human on the Moon and returning them safely to Earth before the end of the decade.

Apollo 11 is therefore not an isolated experiment.

It is the culmination of a larger sequence of Mercury, Gemini, and Apollo missions.

---

# July 16, 1969 - Launch

### 09:32 EDT

Apollo 11 launches from Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center aboard Saturn V AS-506.

The spacecraft enters Earth orbit.

### ~2h 44m after launch

The S-IVB stage performs the Trans-Lunar Injection (TLI) burn.

This places Apollo 11 onto its trajectory toward the Moon.

### After TLI

The Command/Service Module (Columbia) separates from the S-IVB and performs transposition and docking with the Lunar Module (Eagle).

The spacecraft then continues toward the Moon.

---

# July 17 - Translunar Coast

Apollo 11 continues its journey toward the Moon.

A midcourse correction is performed.

The launch was sufficiently accurate that several planned correction burns were ultimately unnecessary.

The crew also conducts television transmissions from space.

---

# July 18 - Lunar Module Checkout

Armstrong and Aldrin enter Eagle from Columbia through the docking tunnel.

They perform checks of the Lunar Module.

Another television transmission is conducted.

---

# July 19 - Lunar Orbit

Apollo 11 passes behind the Moon and temporarily loses communication with Earth.

### ~75h 50m Ground Elapsed Time

The Service Propulsion System performs the Lunar Orbit Insertion burn.

Apollo 11 enters lunar orbit.

Initial orbit:

~69 × 190 miles

The spacecraft later circularizes the orbit.

---

# July 20 - Landing Day

## Before Separation

Armstrong and Aldrin prepare Eagle for descent.

Collins remains aboard Columbia.

---

## Lunar Module Separation

Eagle separates from Columbia.

Collins remains in lunar orbit while Armstrong and Aldrin begin the landing sequence.

---

## Powered Descent

The Lunar Module begins its descent toward the landing site.

The approach does not proceed exactly as planned.

Armstrong takes manual control while searching for a safer landing area after identifying hazardous terrain.

The final landing point is several miles from the original target.

---

## Lunar Landing

### 20 July 1969

Eagle lands in the Sea of Tranquility.

Mission Control receives:

> "The Eagle has landed."

**Important research question:**

How much of the landing sequence was automated versus manually controlled?

---

# July 20-21 - Lunar Surface

Armstrong exits the Lunar Module.

He becomes the first human to step onto the lunar surface.

Aldrin follows.

---

## Activities

Armstrong and Aldrin:

- Photograph the landing site.
- Collect lunar soil and rock samples.
- Deploy scientific equipment.
- Deploy a laser ranging retroreflector.
- Plant the American flag.
- Photograph the Lunar Module and surrounding terrain.
- Collect additional samples.
- Communicate with Mission Control.

---

## Lunar Surface Stay

Total surface stay:

**21 hours, 36 minutes**

Total EVA:

**2 hours, 31 minutes**

---

# July 21 - Lunar Liftoff

### 17:54 EDT

The Lunar Module ascent stage launches from the Moon.

Armstrong and Aldrin leave the lunar surface aboard Eagle's ascent stage.

---

## Rendezvous With Columbia

Eagle enters lunar orbit and performs maneuvers to rendezvous with Columbia.

Armstrong and Aldrin dock with Columbia.

The astronauts transfer back to the Command Module.

The Lunar Module ascent stage is subsequently jettisoned.

---

# July 21 - Trans-Earth Injection

Columbia performs the burn required to leave lunar orbit and begin the journey back to Earth.

The spacecraft passes behind the Moon during the maneuver.

Apollo 11 is now on its return trajectory.

---

# July 22 - Return Flight

A midcourse correction is performed.

Only one correction burn is required during the return journey.

The crew conducts additional television transmissions.

---

# July 24 - Re-entry

Re-entry procedures begin.

The Service Module separates from the Command Module.

The Command Module re-enters Earth's atmosphere.

Parachutes deploy.

---

# July 24 - Splashdown

### ~12:50 EDT

Apollo 11 splashes down in the Pacific Ocean.

The spacecraft lands approximately 13 miles from the recovery ship USS Hornet.

The astronauts are recovered.

---

# Immediate Post-Mission Handling

The Command Module and lunar samples are recovered.

The astronauts enter quarantine procedures.

Lunar samples and mission materials are transported for analysis.

---

# Timeline Questions

These are not conclusions.

They are questions the timeline should help us investigate.

### 1. Independent Tracking

Who was tracking Apollo 11 besides NASA?

### 2. Communications

Were Apollo 11 communications observable or recorded by organizations outside NASA?

### 3. Lunar Landing

What evidence exists for the actual descent and landing?

### 4. Lunar Surface

Which evidence from the surface can be independently verified?

### 5. Physical Objects

What physical objects were left on the Moon?

### 6. Samples

How were the lunar samples handled, analyzed, and authenticated?

### 7. Return

What evidence exists between lunar liftoff and splashdown?

### 8. Human Chain

How many people and organizations would have needed to maintain a consistent false narrative if the mission were fabricated?

---

# Important Distinction

The existence of a detailed timeline does **not** prove that every event happened exactly as described.

It establishes the sequence of claims that need to be tested.

The next step is therefore not:

> "NASA has a timeline, therefore Apollo happened."

The next step is:

> **"Which parts of this timeline have evidence that exists independently of the official narrative?"**
